Over the past month or so I've lamented, both to my family and [in blog form](https://dobrow.blog/blog/apple-developer-experience/), about Apple's shitty developer experience. From submission to approval, it took over three weeks for my app to be distributed live on the app store. The only reason it didn't take longer was because I pulled my original submission and made the app Duo-compatible.

Developers are noticing that Apple is prioritizing apps built to work on the new iPhone Duo. Apps with a Duo badge are approved in days, while normie iPhone app submissions are taking almost a month in some cases. I can understand that Apple wants the Duo to ship with as many apps available a possible, but as I've mentioned in the past, this appears to be a clear understaffing issue. As the most cash rich company on the planet, being understaffed to such a degree is completely unacceptable.

I'm thankful that the [Treasure Coast Today app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/treasure-coast-today/id6821545564) is finally live, but Apple has some serious room for developer experience improvements, and in more ways than just approval times. TestFlight (often broken due to backend issues) and support (tickets go unanswered for weeks) are also equally terrible. Remember, developers are paying customers too. Not just with our $99 annual developer fee, but also 15% of our revenue.
