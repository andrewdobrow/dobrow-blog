[Treasure Coast Today](https://treasurecoast.today) is officially Treasure Coast Today LLC. My first official entity! I'm sure this might sound like a nothing burger, but for me it's a big deal.

I spent some time updating all of the various platforms I use with the new business name, making sure to swap out my personal information for the newly minted business banking account. What a trip.

I expect I'll receive an onslaught of junk mail and spam calls in the near future. In fact, TCT received it's very first junk mail late last week, only a day or so after it was officially approved. It was one of those new business service scams that charge you to do things that are free to do yourself, like registering for an EIN. Those fuckers are quick!
