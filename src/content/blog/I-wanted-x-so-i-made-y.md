title: "The 'I Wanted X, so I Made Y' Curse"
description: "Stop trusting LLMs to write your copy."
pubDate: 2026-11-01
tags: ['marketing']
---

Unlike many, I'm not totally against vibecoding. In fact, it's awesome that AI allows non-technical minded folks to build the projects they've always wanted, but haven't have the time, nor aptitude to learn the required technical skills. There is a cohort of vibecoders who actually do things right. They go beyond the easily identifiable slop that AI typically spits out, and build things that are genuinely helpful. 

Though, I draw the line at AI-written marketing content. Not because I'm against efficiency, but because AI is absolutely terrible at copywriting.

LLMs love "I wanted X, so I made Y." If you ask AI to craft your marketing messaging without any other direction, you're practically guaranteed this regurgitated formula. It seems damn near deterministic. A quick survey of vibecoded product announcements proves just how common this formula has become.

Sounding formulaic is branding poison. We can smell inauthenticity from a mile away. Do yourself a favor and stop using AI to write your copy for you.
