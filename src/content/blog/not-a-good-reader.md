---
title: "I'm Not a Good Reader"
description: "I love reading, but I'm terrible at it."
pubDate: 2026-10-23
tags: ['reading']
---

Reading is one of my great loves. I can't remember a time when it wasn't a part of my existence. But the older I get, the more I realize that I'm terrible at it.

* I'm a chronic DNFer. I start books and let them go too soon. Sometimes within the first 20 pages or so. Time is fleeting and I've grown impatient of books that don't spark joy.

* I overbuy books that I'll never have time for. Being surrounded by books is one my great comforts. At one point in my life I would have considered myself a borderline book hoarder, but now I let them go once I'm done with them.

* I get obsessive about a new series or reading project, only to burn out or realize the project or series wasn't as exciting or fulfilling as I expected.

* I often miss intended themes. I know literary interpretation is subjective to a point, but I often feel like a straight-up dolt when I read about the author's intended meanings. How could I have missed that? I feel like I would get more out of books if I read between the lines, but I often find myself taking the text at face value. This is coming from someone who at one point wanted to be a lit major.

* I wobble between believing reading fiction and reading non-fiction is a waste of time, depending on which I'm reading at the moment. I often feel like I should be spending my time reading something else. Call it reading FOMO.

I've mostly come to terms with my shitty reading habits. Though I fear that there are so many books that maybe, just maybe, if I pressed on a little longer, might be worth the extra effort. But I can't get over the universal truth that time is fleeting, and it should be spent thoughtfully. Wasted time is a tragedy.

Do I really want to commit to eight Dungeon Crawling Carl books when I could be learning something practical or fulfilling? But what is life without laughter and joy? The balance between utilitarian and artistic reading has been a forever struggle for me. But I guess there is enough time for both.
