Over the last few days I've made a major shift in [Treasure Coast Today's](tab:https://treasurecoast.today) monetization strategy.

My initial attempt at monetization involved a combination of a paywall (with a couple free articles per month) and light advertising. I believed that our readers would see the value in our work and shell out a few bucks a month to experience unlimited access to all the articles and an ad-free experience.

While I do think this can be a successful model for a local news site, I believe I erred in making this my initial strategy. I have opted to focus on monetizing via ads, sponsored posts, and paid event listings.

I have not hesitated in my belief that TCT and the engine I've built behind it is worthy of a subscription model, and I still believe it could eventually be a success. But I underestimated how many people REALLY hate paywalls. Like... with a passion. They see paywalls as an afront to their personal freedoms, and they aren't afraid to let you and everyone else on the internet know about it.

It's not only paywall hate that has fueled this shift. I realized that the first step needs to be securing a return readership, which means making the site as accessible as possible. The only people who consider paying for a subscription are going to be the readers who return day after day.

Yeah. Duh. Seems obvious.

We are growing every single day, but much of our traffic is single article-based; social media users seeing a TCT article in their feed. But our metrics show that return users are increasing. I imagine the termination of the paywall will help improve that number even more.

I'm extremely proud of what I've built and how fast we've grown in such a short period of time, especially with several major, long-established competitors. I wake up excited to work every single day, which is more than I could say about almost any other job I've ever had.
